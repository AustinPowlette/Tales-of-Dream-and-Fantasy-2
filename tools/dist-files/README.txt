TALES OF DREAM AND FANTASY 2
============================

A xianxia cultivation world of 100,000 people that lives on without you. You can play one of them.

HOW TO START
- Double-click TalesOfDreamAndFantasy2.exe. A console window opens first (it asks whether you want the map window: answer Y), then the game window.
- No Java needs to be installed: the folder carries its own.
- Keep the whole folder together. The game reads the folders next to the .exe (soundfonts, assets, images, songs) and writes your saves, settings and exports next to it too.
- Move or copy the whole folder to play somewhere else. To start fresh, delete the "saves" folder and "settings.properties".

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. About 4 GB of free memory for a world of 100,000 people (the game caps itself at 3 GB). On a small machine start a smaller world: the game asks for the size when you begin (10,000 to 150,000).
- Sound comes from the SoundFont in the "soundfonts" folder (the music is written for it).

FIRST STEPS
- Type "help" in the console, or press the "?" on any window. The Help window has illustrated pages for everything.
- Under Settings you can turn the music, the lessons, the little stories and the Palace story on or off.
- The game saves by itself (autosave slots) and you can save at the prompt or while paused.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- A file called crash.log appears next to the .exe. Send it with a short note on what you were doing.
