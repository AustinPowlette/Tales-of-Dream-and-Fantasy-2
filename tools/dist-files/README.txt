TALES OF DREAM AND FANTASY 2
============================

A xianxia cultivation world of 100,000 people that lives on without you. You can play one of them.

THINGS TO KNOW
1. When running the exe, you will have to enter "Y" and then press enter for the GUI to pop up

2. I would highly recommend clicking around the different menus before doing anything in the game to get a feel for where everything is first. You can resize menus and windows. If something seems to not be triggering, check your policies and duties.

3. Don't try to do everything manually. The game takes a very long time since it was originally designed to be a simulation. And true to form, cultivations take a long time in general, so learn how to use Hybrid and Auto.

4. The lore requires you to join the sect that exists in Originis (The starting tile). You can do this by doing decently in the tournament held yearly (Also within the first few weeks). You can register for the tournament by selecting the Register for Tournament quest on week 1 or 2 (or 3 or 4?) and clicking Do Quest. Then just wait a few weeks for the tournament to start.
Also, try out some of the character story starts.

5. There is an experimental setting in settings that can potentially decrease the time it takes per tick. This WILL take up most of your CPU... Might also cause occasional music lag.

6. Feedback on Music + Buttons + Ideas accepted
- Which songs do you like / dislike (By name preferably. Hover of On/Off button.)
- Which buttons / tabs could be placed better
- More/less control? Anything you would like added QoL or otherwise?

HOW TO START
- Double-click TalesOfDreamAndFantasy2.exe. A console window opens first (it asks whether you want the map window: answer Y), then the game window.
- No Java needs to be installed: the folder carries its own.
- Keep the whole folder together. The game reads the folders next to the .exe (soundfonts, assets, images, songs) and writes your saves, settings and exports next to it too.
- Move or copy the whole folder to play somewhere else. To start fresh, delete the "saves" folder and "settings.properties".

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. About 4 GB of free memory for a world of 100,000 people (the game caps itself at 3 GB). On a small machine start a smaller world: the game asks for the size when you begin (10,000 to 150,000).
- Sound comes from the SoundFont in the "soundfonts" folder (the music is written for it).

FIRST STEPS
- Type "help" in the console, or press the "?" on any window. The Help window has illustrated pages for everything.
- Under Settings you can turn the music, the lessons, the little stories and the Palace story on or off.
- The game saves by itself (autosave slots) and you can save at the prompt or while paused.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- A file called crash.log appears next to the .exe. Send it with a short note on what you were doing.
