# Start Stories: beat sheets for all three seasons (what was built, 2026-10-19)

> Written while the author slept, after `Start Story Opinions.txt`. The scenes themselves are in `Start Stories Script.md` (generated); this file is the map: premise, mode, voice, the mechanics each season leans on, the chapters, the choices that carry forward. Season 1 of each story is the old story (repaired where the author asked); seasons 2 and 3 are new.
> **Pacing rule used by all new chapters:** no chapter waits more than about six years beyond its gap for a Major level or a wish (`Chapters.PATIENCE`); a "reach Major N" task is waived after eight years (`REALM_WAIVER`); gaps are weeks to two years, a season is eight to eleven chapters. **Romance rule:** every person who may be loved is always of the other gender than the player (`Cast.opposite`); text uses `{key.he}` / `{key.him}` / `{key.his}`.
> **Length, honestly:** the three seasons of a story run 10,500 to 14,900 words (about 4,300 to 7,100 a season in Seasons 2 and 3 as counted in the script file, scaffolding included; 9 to 13 chapters a season); Seasons 2 and 3 reach about the 5,000 a season the author asked for (script-file count; the prose alone is about 15% less); only Season 1 of the Disciple, Lucky Fool and Ghost (the originals, kept short) is under it. The longest is the Smith; the Orphan has the most road tales. Chapters are counted in `Start Stories Script.md`. See `Seasons Work Log.md` for what is left.
