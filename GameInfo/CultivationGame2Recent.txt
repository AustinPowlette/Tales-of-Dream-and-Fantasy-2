--- Design Decisions (2026-09-19) ---
Technique rarity tiers: RESOLVED as implicit. Technique potential (1-100) already encodes
rarity — a technique with potential 80 is naturally rare since few characters can generate
one. No separate Rare/Legendary tier system needed; the potential value IS the rarity signal.
Actual rarity emerges from the world: high-potential techniques require high-potential
crafters (Innovative trait), and are gatekept behind sectPoints in sect libraries.

Event system cases 0/3/4 (PvP battle, Forage, Mine): REMOVED from event(). Aggression is
now exclusively quest-driven (Battle Dislike/Rival/Arch-Enemy/Revenge from changeRelationship
and dead()). Forage/Mine are exclusive profession spot quests, not random events. Event
weights redistributed: beast 7-15%, interact 30-40%, profession 35-40%, loot 13-20%.

Sect mission delivery: requires physical return to sect location. Character collects materials
via Forage/Mine spot quests; on reaching threshold, "Deliver Materials" target quest fires
to sect.location; treasury updated on arrival. Simple two-phase pattern.

City taxation: 1% of city population as yearly ss income to cityOwner. Scales with city
growth; complements shop revenue and tribute income.

City/sect destruction: triggered from winCheck() when Evil or Beast kills at an array-free
city/sect (20% chance). Attacker plunders all bags, gains Blood = population/100, +10
cultivate() calls, 10% chance +1 potential, ss += population, population drops to 10%.
Sect dissolved (members lose sect, treasury cleared). City cleared (city=false, shops/libs
removed). Originis is hardcoded as indestructible. Rewards scale with city size.

Hunt for Money: when NPC visits a city shop but still lacks weapon or armor slot after
buyGear() (can't afford), queues "Hunt Beast" at priority 40 toward nearest monster area.

Lucky one-time loot gate: when a corpse within 30 levels appears in Lucky's tile, a
one-time "Loot Corpse" spot quest fires at priority 75 (overrides normal profession). This
is reactive and per-corpse, not a permanent state change.

True Immortal rebirth: moved from toString() side-effect to proper dead(killer) early-return
guard. Rebirth resets dead=false, time=0, age=0; killer still gets kill credit.

Monster NPC awakening (IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-19): 10% chance in winCheck() when Beast kills
a cultivator (loser.dead==true, loser.trait!=Beast). awakenMonster() flips trait to Monster,
sets affiliation=Evil, assigns legendary title prefix (Ancient/Dread/Elder/Primordial/Titan/
Sovereign/Infernal), gets id=UID++, age=8, enters characters[] via direct add + awakenedMonsters
list (removed from monstersList at end of tick without population count impact). Initializes
with monsterQuestInitializer(): Hunt(60) + Hunt Cultivator(55) + Random(25) — no profession/gear
economy. Earns kill-milestone title suffixes in dead(): Legend@10, Tyrant@20, Dread Lord@30,
Immortal Terror@50. Monsters are excluded from gearNeeded/Seek Potential/Buy Pills triggers.

Monster AI depth (IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-19 Batch 16):
- Hunt Cultivator spot quest: iterates location.characters, calls battleCheck() on first non-Beast
  non-Monster alive target. Gives Monsters active cultivator aggression beyond just relationship quests.
- Seek Territory target quest: 20%/turn trigger in quest(); seeks highest-lvl known area with
  lvl > current.lvl+10; calls beastBattleCheck() on arrival. Drives Monster expansion into
  progressively more dangerous zones.
- Cultivation XP from kills: in dead() cultivator-vs-cultivator block, Monsters get
  Math.min(victim.lvl, 20) cultivate() calls. Monsters grow from PvP combat.
- Legendary milestone enhancements: +10 cultivate() bonus calls, 5% chance +1 potential, and
  post Bounty Hunt (priority 65) at nearest city via cityAreas. City cultivators within 10 levels
  pick up the bounty via the existing Bounty Hunt pickup in quest().
- Sect Hunt Beast missions: postMissionsYearly() posts "Hunt|Beast|lvl|qty|bonus" when sl>=5.
  The "Hunt" spot quest case decrements the remaining qty on each completion; grants sectMissionBonus
  when qty hits 0. No delivery phase needed (kills are instant rewards).

Depth mechanics batch (IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-19 Batch 18):
- Breakthrough Tribulation: fires at majorLvl 3/5/7/9 in cultivate(). Pass chance =
  clamp(10,90, potential - (majorLvl-3)*5). Pass: cultivationBase += 0.5*max, 10% +1 potential.
  Fail: majorLvl reverts, inhibitor++. Adds meaningful risk to major realm crossings.
- Dual Cultivation: 5%/turn in quest(). Spouse in tile → 3 cultivate() each. Best friend
  (value≥3) in tile → 1 cultivate() each. Encourages staying near loved ones.
- Legacy Titles: dead() records name+lvl in Map.legacyTitles/legacyLvls for majorLvl≥7 deaths.
  quest() has 2%/turn Legacy Discovery: min(legLvl/10, 10) cultivate() calls. Historical
  legends leave mechanical echoes in the world.
- Celestial Trials: yearly event spawns CELESTIAL_TRIAL dungeon at random wilderness lvl≥20 area
  for 10 ticks. Notifies characters within 15 tiles via knownLocations. On completion: 3 boss
  fights, 20 cultivate(), 25% +1 potential, 10% technique. Timer ticks down in GameDriver loop.
- Beast Tides: yearly check. BEAST_LAIR monsters > lvl*2000 overflow to 4 neighbors.
  City neighbors lose up to 10% population. Gives Beast Lairs world impact.
- Heaven-Sent Treasure: every 3 years a high-potential technique (potential 50-100, "Heaven-Sent"
  prefix) appears in a random wilderness area (area.heavenlyTechnique). Broadcasts to chars within
  20 tiles. "Heaven Treasure" priority-70 target quest; first arrival claims it.
- Weapon Enchantments: Innovative 5%/turn chance to enchant equipped weapon in professionPicker().
  Type: Sharp/Spiritual/Swift/Heavy/Fierce/Wrathful/Sundering. Bonus +1–5 to enchantBonus.
  Applied in tempLvlCheck(). Shown in Item.toString() as [Type +N].
- Trade Caravan growth: Go Sell / Sell Stock success adds earnings/1M to city.population.
  Reflects merchants driving city growth.
- Rob Caravan quest: Evil chars, 5%/turn, target Merchant in same tile with ss≥1000.
  Priority-50 target quest; calls partyBattleCheck(merchant) on arrival.
- Hotspot faction wars: squabble += 2 for hotspot areas (vs 1 for dungeons); war threshold
  lowered from 5 to 4. Hotspot areas escalate conflicts faster.
- MapViewer GUI: optional Swing JFrame (Y/n at startup). MapPanel reads Map.tiles[][] each tick.
  Color-codes by biome/feature. Click to select tile; info panel shows stats, top chars, bounties.
  Refreshes via SwingUtilities.invokeLater() at end of each game tick. Future: area image view
  and cultivator/beast sprite overlays (planned, not started).


[2026-09-28 economy/parties]
- Cities and sects founded at Major Lvl 2 (Leaders); ownerless cities (e.g. Originis) still function as markets.
- Beast loot follows item price scale (lvl^2.5*10*(1+pot/100)); city owners earn yearly tax only (pop/500, half to city treasury).
- Cultivators hunt beasts from own major realm down to 2 realms below; same-realm hunts form parties (friends + non-hostile strangers within 1 realm); parties persist across repeated hunts.
- Buy Gear travel only when a shop stocks an affordable upgrade (avoids everyone clustering at the shop city).

[2026-09-29 quest queue / cities / population]
- Quest queue: repeating quests release after each success; priorities age via Character.bump() (clamped to maxPriority, heap rebuilt). Social quests sit in the lowest band (10-48): Like/Dislike capped at 4 one-time each and replaced by a different cultivator of the same relationship value; Friend/Close Friend/Rival/Arch-Enemy persist. Target queue hard-capped at 50; one-time quests expire after 10 years.
- Spot quest "Test" replaced by "Meditate" (extra cultivation tick, low priority).
- City level = clamp(floor((treasury/10)^0.4 / 10), 1, 10); 3 shops per city level; shop level = min(city level, owner major realm) and stocks items up to level*10.
- Population: tiles over 100 residents (cities 400) lose 30% of the excess yearly (births untouched, after year 10); at 200k living, 10% of each major level dies in batches (levels under 10 spared).

[2026-09-29 crowd hotspots / city economy]
- Overcrowding: a tile over capacity (100, cities 400, after year 10) becomes a hotspot; beasts ambush its cultivators each tick regardless of level and kill any they defeat, until the tile is under 80% of capacity. Cultivators there get a "Disperse" quest to a quieter nearby tile. Births are never reduced.
- Sect raids: one per sect per 13 weeks. City population is bounded (cap 100k x cityLevel^2, 0.1%/tick growth); yearly tax 1% of population.

[2026-09-29 player control]
- Human characters have three modes: MANUAL (commands only), AUTO (NPC pipeline + decision policies), HYBRID (script). Info/setup commands cost no time.
- Quests are classified routine / alert / duty. MANUAL ignores routine ones, announces alerts (target, coordinates, distance, reason), and runs duty quests only when that duty toggle is on. Ten duty toggles plus a master all on/off.
- Player-set quest priorities are pinned (never age, clamp, expire or get shed); deleted quests can be muted so triggers do not repost them; "Do Quest" runs a queued quest only at its target tile.
- Kill or spare (spare = don't kill) is asked only for cultivators; flee, forbidden technique and duel acceptance are also decisions; non-MANUAL characters use policies (default: spare, flee, no forbidden technique, accept duels).
- Players set their shops' buy/sell/restock rates within safe bounds; "Get Pregnant" is displayed as "Have Child".

[2026-09-30 crowd threshold / auctions]
- Crowd capacity = 2% of the STARTING population scaled by map area (31x31 reference), cities 4x, floor 20; never scales with the current population. Originis is exempt from crowd hotspots.
- Humans in a city bid by hand at the yearly auction (max bid per lot, winner pays runner-up); prompts can time out to the policy answer (`policy timeout`).


== QoL pass 2026-09-30 (design decisions) ==
- Concurrency: game thread owns World.lock, released at every wait + end of tick; GUI reads via tryLock(timeout) and skips a refresh rather than block; GUI never writes game state (queues commands).
- Saves: newest autosave is auto-1 (auto-0 is only the temp write before rotation); every save keeps a .bak; load falls back to .bak when the CRC fails; crash writes crash-<time>.
- Confirmations: risky commands ask (default No); "!cmd" = already confirmed; script/AUTO commands count as confirmed (written on purpose). GUI asks itself, then sends "!cmd".
- Level-up notices start muted; the recap fires every 52 weeks by default (0 = off).
- Control switch keeps the duty/policy setup (inheritFrom); the old character becomes a tracked NPC (player == 2).

[2026-09-30 balance decisions]
- Leveling is meant to be brutal and slow (perfect cultivator ~2,600 yrs to Major 10 is fine); most cultivators stay low, few reach the top via potential, time, luck or wealth. Do not speed up the XP curve.
- Early massacre too brutal: aim for about a 33% population drop at most. NPCs and monsters must be able to reach top realms.
- inhibitor = injury from a failed breakthrough; a low-potential failure soft-caps growth. Money: scarce and plentiful at once; gear one Major above costs a significant sum; money flows (beast loot -> trades); prices and income follow the same polynomial curve as leveling.
