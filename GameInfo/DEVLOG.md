## 2026-10-16 (beta tester's list, batch C: the five elements)
- Added: **Elements** (`Elements.java`). Every creature (cultivators, beasts, monsters) has a cultivation **path** of one element - Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water - with a name ("Way of the Silent Mountain", `Character.path`, one `short`: element, Path/Way/Dao/Art, adjective, noun). It is made from the creature's id when first asked (never from the game's random numbers), so old saves and every existing world get stable paths and nothing else about a world changed; a newborn follows a parent's element half the time (`Elements.inherit`, in the birth constructor). The two cycles are the author's: overcoming (Wood parts Earth, Earth absorbs Water, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal chops Wood) and generating (Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal holds Water, Water nourishes Wood).
- Added: every **technique** has an element (`Technique.elemPlus1` / `element()`): a new one takes its maker's element 55% of the time (else any), the name always carries a word of it (`Naming.technique(type, maker, element)`, `Elements.TECH_WORDS`: Bamboo/Willow = Wood, Flame/Ember = Fire, Stone/Mountain = Earth, Iron/Silver = Metal, River/Tide = Water), a copy keeps it, an old save's technique gets one from its name, the Default Technique has none and follows whoever uses it. `Technique.toString`, the Techniques tab and `techniques` show it.
- Changed: **fights** - `winChance += Elements.swing(self, opponent)` at the three places `battleCore` computes it and in `previewFight`. A fighter's edge = two thirds the martial technique + one third the movement technique, each = (the technique's element against the other's path: overcomes +8, overcome -8, drains +3, feeds -3) + (the technique in its owner's hands: attuned +5, feeds the owner's path +3, the owner's path feeds it +2, holds the owner's path back -6, the owner's path holds it back -4); the swing is a's edge minus b's edge, in percentage points (one point of fighting power = 15). A beast fights with its own element. Two defaults of different elements swing +/-16 in the overcomer's favour (measured: Fire beats equal Metal 65%). Not applied to the estimates in `Area` dungeon clears, `Lore` and `SectPolitics`, nor to `Rankings.fightRating`.
- Added: **where it shows** - the Stats sheet and `stats` (path and element), the character card, the people card (path, and the cycle between you and them), the fight preview (an "Elements" line and `elements +N` in the one-liner), the fight report ("Elements: your Fire path against Wood's (Fire ...): +N points"), the Edit Character dialog (a PATH box with the five elements and a note with the words; creation only; Randomize rolls it; it is fixed for life afterwards), the Help page "The five elements" and a lesson "The five elements". `Elements.COLOR` colours the element names.
- Not done (on purpose): NPC fighters do not choose their technique against the target (they keep using their strongest of each kind); terrain and weather do not touch the elements.
- Added: probe `ElementsProbe` (headless, 23): the paths, the cycles as given, the table, the swing and its symmetry, techniques, children, the displays; Fire beats equal Metal about 65% in 1,500 real fights.
