# Story voice cards (as used, 2026-10-19)

> The cards from `Story Writing Framework.md` section 3, filled in from the author's samples (`Postulate.txt`, `Postulate Planning.txt`, `Catch One.txt`, `Offworld Side Stories.txt`, `Side Stories Planning.txt`) and the author's notes in `Start Story Opinions.txt` ("wordplay mainly in the comic modes"; "no AI-isms"; "do not copy the first-person/third-person switch of Postulate"). Checked by `tools/story_lint.py`.

## What the author's pages teach (beyond the framework's first analysis)
- **Catch One** (a lockdown diary that turns into a fight with a ghoul): long, running sentences that pile up images and then deflate them with a joke ("hard-boiled detective... literally being hard-boiled"); puns on idiom ("pick the booger", "Mr. Fahrenheit"); a narrator who argues with himself ("Bah, it needs work"); a mock-serious tone about a small thing; time stamps ("about 2 hours later"); cause and effect told as a chain of mishaps.
- **Postulate Planning**: rules for powers (a field, a limit, a price), a cast with a one-line hook each, a world with procedures (the government offers free training to monitor you), a detective who cannot say no to a crying lady.
- **Side Stories Planning**: myth built from a few concrete things (a canoe, a straw hat, a crescent moon, a clock ticking, "we don't even get the privilege of shadows"), ores with uses, folklore that is wrong in a useful way (a thrown stone does not ward the monster, it makes it like you).

## The lint (hard bans, zero in new text)
"It is not X. It is Y."; "You do not X. You Y."; "for a long time / some time / a while"; "in the end"; "a long breath"; "the weight of"; "heart/chest/breath" reactions; "warm/soft/small smile"; every em dash; any sentence over 40 words (the story test also fails at 40). **Watchlist** per 1,000 words: small/tiny/little 2.0, very/really/quite 1.0, quiet/soft/gentle 0.5, warm/cozy 0.3, named emotions 2.5. The new seasons are over on most of these still; the average sentence is 10 to 12 words with a wide spread (the old text: 8 with half the sentences under 8).
